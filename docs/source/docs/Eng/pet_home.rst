Pet home Page
-------------

**Pet home** — the first page. Your saved pets move into a small home, live there on their own and work for home coins.

* *Create a home* gives a bed, a dining table, a writing desk and 10 coins. Nothing is created or started until you press it, and every other tool works without a home.
* *Move in* a saved pet or make a *New pet*. A pet lives either in the home or on the desktop, never both at once; its energy, fullness, mood and affection carry over and go back to its profile when it moves out.
* *Arrange* puts furniture from storage on the floor. A red preview says why a spot does not work (outside the home, something already there, blocks the entrance); Esc stops arranging. *Put away* returns a piece to storage, and work at it pauses with its progress kept.
* *Work* gives the selected resident a job, such as writing letters at the desk (2 minutes and 12 coins each). Pets walk to the desk, work, get tired, sleep, eat and come back by themselves; each finished unit pays exactly once, and a cancelled one pays nothing.
* *Shop* sells extra furniture; selling returns at most half the price, and the basic bed, table and desk are never sold.
* Time passes only while the home is running (*Start* / *Pause*); time while FrontEngine was closed or the computer slept is never simulated. The home is saved to ``pet-home.json`` beside your settings, with the previous good copy kept as ``pet-home.json.bak``. A save that cannot be read, or one from a newer version, is never overwritten: the page offers the backup, or keeps the old file and starts a new home.
